The daisies from the team shirt, traced from a photograph of it. Each file is a single flat ink, shown with `<img>`, so pick the file for the ground instead of recolouring.

- `daisies-*.svg` is the pair, as printed on the shirt (400 × 277). This is the logo.
- `daisy-a-*.svg` is the left daisy on its own (234 × 217), and `daisy-b-*.svg` the right one (192 × 190). On the shirt the two overlap by one petal each with no line drawn between them; here each daisy has its own petal, closed at the tip. Use them in the home page field and as a small accent, not as the logo.

Each comes in three inks:

- `-ink` is `ink` (#111111). Use it on `paper` and `chalk`. This is the default.
- `-white` is `on-navy` (#ffffff). Use it on `navy`.
- `-navy` is `navy` (#1c2452). Use it for the home page field.

Keep the proportions. Do not show the pair under 48px wide or a single under 32px. Replace all of them with the original artwork when it is available.
